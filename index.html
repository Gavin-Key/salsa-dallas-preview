Questions people ask before their first class

Do I need a partner?

No. Most people come alone. We rotate partners during class so everyone practices with everyone.

I’ve never danced. Is that OK?

That’s exactly who the beginner course is for. Many students start out nervous; the group is friendly and goes step by step.

What if I miss the first class of the month?

Because everyone in a group learns together, new students have to start on day one. If you miss it, you’ll be moved to next month’s group. Already enrolled and miss a later class? Ask Luis about a make-up class.

Tuesday or Thursday, which is better?

They’re the same beginner course. Pick the night that works. Tuesday is followed by bachata at 8 PM; Thursday by Intermediate/Advanced salsa.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and shoes that turn easily (avoid sneakers with heavy grip). No dance shoes required.

Can I pay cash?

Please register online first so we can save your spot. If you’ve already paid in cash, don’t pay again; just text us and we’ll confirm.
